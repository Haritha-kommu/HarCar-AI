================================================================================
DATASET INFORMATION: USED CAR PRICE PREDICTION
================================================================================

1. DATASET OVERVIEW & SOURCE:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dataset Name: CarDekho Used Car Dataset (car_data.csv)
- Primary Source: Scraped from CarDekho.com, publicly archived on Kaggle / GitHub 
  (Open data for educational and academic research purposes).
- Domain: Indian Automotive Secondary Market
- Total Records (Rows): 301 car listings
- Total Attributes (Columns): 9 features

2. COLUMN DESCRIPTIONS & UNITS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Car_Name:
   - Type: Categorical (String / Object)
   - Description: The brand and model name of the car (e.g., ritz, sx4, ciaz, swift, ertiga, city, etc.)

2. Year:
   - Type: Numerical (Integer)
   - Description: The manufacturing / registration year of the car (Range: 2003 to 2018).
   - Usage in ML: Can be used to derive 'Car_Age' (Current_Year - Year).

3. Selling_Price (TARGET VARIABLE):
   - Type: Numerical (Float, Continuous)
   - Description: The actual price at which the car is listed/sold in the secondary market.
   - Unit: Indian Rupees in Lakhs (1 Lakh = 100,000 INR). Example: 3.35 represents ₹3,35,000.

4. Present_Price:
   - Type: Numerical (Float, Continuous)
   - Description: The ex-showroom price of the vehicle when purchased new.
   - Unit: Indian Rupees in Lakhs (1 Lakh = 100,000 INR). Example: 5.59 represents ₹5,59,000.

5. Kms_Driven:
   - Type: Numerical (Integer)
   - Description: Total odometer distance traveled by the car.
   - Unit: Kilometers (km).

6. Fuel_Type:
   - Type: Categorical (Nominal)
   - Categories: Petrol, Diesel, CNG
   - Description: Fuel system mechanism powering the vehicle.

7. Seller_Type:
   - Type: Categorical (Nominal)
   - Categories: Dealer (Authorized or multi-brand car dealership), Individual (Private owner selling directly).

8. Transmission:
   - Type: Categorical (Nominal)
   - Categories: Manual, Automatic
   - Description: Gearbox transmission configuration.

9. Owner:
   - Type: Numerical (Integer)
   - Values: 0 (First Owner), 1 (Second Owner), 3 (Fourth Owner or above)
   - Description: Number of previous registered owners.

3. SERVICE HISTORY STATUS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Status: NOT PRESENT
- Assessment: The dataset does NOT contain service records, periodic maintenance logs, accident history, or warranty status.
- ML Impact: Physical condition and maintenance wear must be approximated using proxy features: 'Car_Age', 'Kms_Driven', and 'Owner'.

4. DATASET LIMITATIONS & PROJECT CONSIDERATIONS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Sample Size:
   - With 301 records, the dataset is compact and suitable for academic mini-projects and demonstrations, but larger datasets (e.g., 4000+ records) are preferred for national-scale production models.
2. Temporal Window:
   - Data collection reflects market conditions up to 2018; prices should be evaluated relative to that economic period.
3. Geographical Context:
   - Prices and depreciation rates are tailored specifically to the Indian automotive market (measured in Lakhs INR).
4. Car Name High Cardinality:
   - Many unique car model names exist with few instances each; feature engineering (e.g. brand extraction or categorical encoding) will be required.
5. Missing Qualitative Factors:
   - Attributes like vehicle cosmetic condition, accidental status, insurance validity, tire health, and exact city tier are not recorded.
